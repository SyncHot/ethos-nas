# Third-party notices

EthOS NAS bundles **no** third-party JavaScript, no fonts and no image assets:
wallpapers and icons are generated (CSS gradients, `scripts/make_icons.py`,
hand-authored SVG in `frontend/static/core/*`). Libraries are loaded from CDNs
at runtime and remain under their own licenses.

## Data

- **Radio stations** (`backend/radio_stations.json`) are generated by
  `scripts/build_radio_catalog.py` from
  [radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info) (public domain) plus a
  curated EthOS list. Station logos are hotlinked from the broadcasters, not
  redistributed.
- **Podcasts** (`backend/podcast_catalog.json`) are generated by
  `scripts/build_podcast_catalog.py` from the public iTunes Search/Lookup API
  (country PL) plus a curated list. Show titles, descriptions and artwork
  belong to their publishers and are shown for identification only.
- **Video metadata** uses the TMDB API. This product uses the TMDB API but is
  not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Set your own key in
  Videos settings.

## System packages

The installer (`setup_scripts/install.sh`) installs distribution packages
(Samba, ffmpeg, nftables, smartmontools, 7zip, rclone, …) from Ubuntu's
repositories. They are used through their CLIs and are not redistributed with
this project.

## EthOS packages

Apps installed through Packages (e.g. Video, Photos, Music, Docker Manager,
Download Manager, Kanban) live in the separate
[ethos-packages](https://github.com/SyncHot/ethos-packages) repository, each
with its own MIT license and manifest.
